About SatyaCheck

How Our Trust Engine Works

  • A single report is a sighting, never an automatic verdict.
  • Coordinated bot accounts on the same network or with copy-pasted notes are merged into a single cohort.
  • An identifier reaches Known Scam only when multiple independent people on distinct networks corroborate it.
  • Numbers are masked until enough independent sightings exist, preventing harassment.
  • Legitimate businesses and helplines can verify their identity or dispute incorrect reports.

Our Privacy Architecture

  • We never ask for your name, phone number, or exact GPS coordinates.
  • Your device and network are pseudonymised using HMAC-SHA256 salted tokens.
  • Notes are automatically scrubbed for personal details and strictly capped at 300 characters.
  • No screenshots are ever uploaded to our servers.
